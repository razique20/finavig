# Finavig Logo

## Overview

The Finavig logo is the visual identity used across the app, onboarding flow, and web presence. It is a single, transparent mark designed to sit cleanly on both light and dark surfaces without a surrounding background.

## File used in the app

- **Path:** `assets/New Logo/finavig-mark-transparent.png`
- **Type:** Transparent PNG
- **Used on:** Welcome screen, login screen, and other UI surfaces where the brand appears as a small icon or header mark.

## Meaning

The logo represents the core idea behind Finavig: a single, steady point of guidance for finances and documents.

The name **Finavig** combines ideas of:

- **Finance:** money, cash flow, budgets, and payment awareness.
- **Navigation / vigilance:** keeping track of what matters, noticing deadlines, and staying ahead of expiry and renewal moments.

In that sense, the logo is meant to read as a marker of **orientation and oversight** — a small visual cue that says, "this is where you keep track of the things that need attention."

## Role in the app

- **Identity cue:** the logo is shown on the welcome and login screens so users immediately recognize the app's brand before they start using it.
- **Trust cue:** in an app that handles sensitive documents and financial information, the logo also acts as a visual anchor for legitimacy and consistency.
- **Navigation cue:** the logo is tappable on the login screen, linking back to the welcome screen, so it also serves as a small return point in the onboarding flow.

## Design intent

- Transparent background so it can be placed on any surface.
- Simple enough to work at small sizes (icon scale) and still feel like the same brand.
- Clean and modern, matching the app's overall tone: calm, organized, and focused on tracking what matters.

## Practical notes

- When placing the logo, keep it legible at the intended size.
- On dark themes, make sure it sits on a surface where the mark is still readable.
- Do not add a solid background behind the logo unless the design intentionally calls for one; the asset is intended to be transparent.
