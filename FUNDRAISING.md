## 1. The story in one paragraph (investor version)

> GCC businesses lose real money to missed renewals — trade licences,
> Emirates IDs, visas, insurance — and track them in WhatsApp and shoebox
> receipts. Global fintech ignores this wedge; regional tools ignore
> either documents or money, never both. Finavig combines document-deadline
> tracking with cash-flow management and AI (scan → AI extraction,
> one-sentence money logging, 90-day forecast), is local-first with a
> Supabase cloud sync, and monetizes via Free / Plus AED 25 / Business
> AED 99 tiers — with a phased, partner-first fintech roadmap (payments →
> wallet → credit) where every tracked document is a guaranteed recurring
> payment intent. Pilot economics are tiny (~$125/yr infra); the ask is
> growth capital, not survival money.

**Traction framing available today:** shipped production app (iOS/Android,
14-screen tour), 338 passing tests, working AI features, freemium tiers
live in code, pitch + screenshots ready (`PITCH.md`).

Sheraa, Tamkeen, TAQADAM, in5). Stack grants + accelerator before selling
equity — often enough to reach seed metrics.

## 4. How to approach each type

### Universal rules
1. **Warm intro > cold email > application form.** Ask every founder you
   know: *"Who do you know at [fund]?"* — one line, one target, easy yes.
2. **Never blast.** 8–12 funds max per sprint (valu.vc's advice is right).
   A fund that can't lead your stage wastes both sides' time.
3. **Lead with traction, not idea.** Finavig's edge in outreach: the app
   *exists* — screenshots, tests, working AI. Most pitches are decks.
4. **One-page teaser first**, 12-slide deck on request, data room when
   they ask.
5. **Weekly momentum:** batch meetings into 2–3 week sprints; create
   deadline pressure ("lead term sheet by [date]").

### By target type
| Type | Best door | What they screen for |
|---|---|---|
| **MENA VCs** | Founder intro or event; else cold email to partner (not info@) | Team, GCC wedge clarity, why-now, early usage |
| **Angel networks** | Apply to pitch night (DAI, DTA, Falak) | Coachability, round co-invest structure |
| **Accelerators** | Online application (rolling/cohort) | Founder quality, product momentum |
| **Equity-free programs** | Application + residency willingness | Job creation, UAE/KSA anchoring |
| **Corporate programs** | Application via their portal | Pilot-ability with their clients |
| **Global fintech funds** | Warm intro strongly preferred | Inclusion thesis, unit economics |

### Where to meet them in person (2026 circuit)
- **Seamless Middle East** (Dubai, May) — the region's biggest fintech event
- **24 Fintech** (Riyadh, Sept) — Saudi's flagship fintech gathering
- **Fintech Abu Dhabi** (ADGM, Nov) — regulator + bank heavy
- **GITEX Expand North Star** (Dubai, Oct) — startup + investor density
- **Step Conference** (Dubai, Feb) — consumer tech + VCs
- **LEAP** (Riyadh, Feb) — tech + sovereign capital

## 6. Metrics to have ready (data-room checklist)

**Product:** MAU/retention D30, docs tracked, renewals surfaced (and
*estimated fines prevented* — your money slide), transactions logged
monthly, % via voice/NLP, scan→extraction accuracy.

**Revenue:** registered → paid conversion (target 2–5%), MRR, ARPU by
tier, Business collections count (your AED 99 ARPU proof), churn.

**Distribution:** CAC if spending, organic %, App Store rating, waitlist,
PRO/accountant pipeline.

**Ops:** infra cost/user (~pennies — Supabase pilot economics), AI cost
per summary (Groq pricing), support load.

**Fintech Phase 1 prep:** which document types renew most often (your
first payment verticals), survey results "would you pay this renewal
here?", PSP conversations (Checkout.com, Network International, Telr,
PayTabs, Stripe).

**Documents:** cap table, UAE/Freezone entity docs, IP assignment,
T&Cs/Privacy (live), roadmap (`FINTECH_ROADMAP.md`), this file's round
model.

|---|---|
| 1 | Fix ask + milestone; build teaser + deck (reuse `PITCH.md`); line up data room |
| 2 | Shortlist 10–12 funds from §3 (mix: 3 VCs, 3 angels, 3 programs, 2 corporate); collect intros |
| 3 | Send wave 1 (emails + intro requests); submit Hub71 / Flat6Labs / Antler apps |
| 4 | First calls; weekly update goes out; submit equity-free applications (Sheraa, TAQADAM) |
| 5 | Partner meetings; event circuit booked (next conference on list) |
| 6 | Push to term sheet; circulate "closing soon" signal; start wave 2 only if needed |

**Rule:** accelerators + equity-free run in parallel always — they're
application-flow, not negotiation-flow, and strengthen the equity story.

## 7. Objections → answers

| Objection | Answer |
|---|---|
| "Solo founder?" | Builder ships production quality (338 tests, CI gates); hiring plan funded by round; advisory board filling |
| "Why will SMEs pay?" | Fines >> subscription price (one missed licence > year of FV); Business tier targets PROs/accountants with highest ARPU |
| "Competition?" | Global apps ignore renewals; document managers ignore money; the combined wedge + GCC authority catalog is the moat |
| "Payments regulation risk?" | Partner-first: licensed PSP takes custody; Finavig is facilitator (documented in FINTECH_ROADMAP guardrails) |
| "Market too small?" | KSA+UAE = region's two largest VC markets; wedge expands: payments → wallet → credit on renewal data |
| "AI cost will kill margins?" | Pilot econ ~$125/yr infra; AI metered by quota; Edge-Function migration planned pre-scale |
