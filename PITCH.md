Every screenshot below is the live app running with demo data.

---

## Why

Every GCC company juggles the same three failures — and the tools that should help were built for someone else.

**Missed renewals mean fines.** Trade licences, Emirates IDs, visas, insurance, Mulkiya. They live in WhatsApp threads and desk drawers, and every missed date converts straight into government fines and blocked services.

**Money is scattered.** Receipts in shoeboxes, salaries in one sheet, supplier payments in another. Nobody can answer "what do we actually have left this month?" without an hour of archaeology.

**Generic apps do not fit.** Global finance apps ignore renewals entirely. Document managers ignore money. GCC businesses need both in one place, speaking AED and Arabic-name reality.

One missed trade licence can cost more than a year of FV — in fines alone.

---

## How

**1. Scan or add a document.** Point the camera at a trade licence, Emirates ID or visa. Files stay on your phone.

**2. AI reads it for you.** Expiry date, renewal fee, vendor and document type are extracted automatically.

**3. Log money in one sentence.** "Spent 85 AED on Uber." FV picks the category, date and currency. Recurring bills log themselves.

**4. Get warned, then relax.** Escalating reminders before every deadline, budget warnings as you approach limits, and a 90-day cash forecast.

Under the hood: local-first storage. Your documents and records live on your device and sync to your private cloud account — fast offline access, no vendor lock-in, one-tap data export. We never sell your data or train public AI models on it.
